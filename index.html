Job Overview

At Nexus Systems, our Security Research Lab safeguards foundational infrastructure supporting millions of mission-critical transactions daily. As a Systems & Security Research Engineer, you will operate at the intersection of low-level OS internals, microarchitectural resilience, and cutting-edge distributed systems defense.

You will collaborate with world-class cryptographers, compiler engineers, and security analysts to probe vulnerabilities, prototype defensive kernel runtime primitives, and publish research findings that establish industry standards.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct in-depth vulnerability analysis, fuzzing, and penetration testing on operating system kernels, hypervisors, and hardware isolation layers.
  • Architect automated threat detection instrumentation and secure sandboxing mechanisms for high-throughput cloud environments.
  • Develop proof-of-concept exploits and defensive mitigations to protect next-generation containerized microservices and Kubernetes clusters.
  • Author high-fidelity research documentation, RFCs, and academic publications, representing Nexus Systems at premier conferences.

Qualifications & Experience

Minimum Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s, Master’s, or equivalent practical experience in Computer Science, Cybersecurity, or related STEM discipline.
  • Demonstrated proficiency in low-level systems programming languages (C/C++, Rust) and scripting/automation with Python.
  • Solid understanding of Linux kernel internals, virtual memory management, system calls, and network protocol stacks (TCP/IP, TLS).

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with eBPF, secure enclave technology (Intel SGX, AWS Nitro Enclaves), or hypervisor virtualization primitives.
  • Prior contributions to open-source security tools or recognized vulnerability disclosures (CVEs).
